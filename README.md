# Coloor
___
# It is not final. We are not even an fifth of a way there.
___
This is our game "Coloor". It is currently in the development process. For the first ship. You can move, change colors, shoot, jump, and dash. Use "A" and "D" for move; "Q" and "E" to dash; "W" to jump; and "S" to shoot. Also you can open the color changing menu when you press "Tab" or press the button on your screen.

You can break glasses with the bullets.

Have fun and wish us luck!
